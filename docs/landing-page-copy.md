# Ismoo landing page: copy for review

*Every word a visitor can see on ismoo.tech, in page order, as of 29 October 2026. Text in italics is a reviewer's note and does not appear on the page: where a line sits, how it behaves, and what changes between phone and desktop. Everything else is live copy, word for word.*

*What changed since the last review:*

- *New rotating phrases in the hero, and a new line under the headline: "Your tools should make that easier, not harder."*
- *The doors section has a new heading and intro: "Meeting you where you are".*
- *Our approach has a new heading and intro: "Built for you. With you."*
- *Door 3 (AI) has a new opening.*
- *There are now three doors. "Something's off" and "It works today. Will it work at twice the size?" were removed, and a "Ready to Scale? Not sure where to start?" line follows the doors instead.*
- *Booking now happens on the page: visitors pick a time in the form and get a calendar invite.*

*What to review:*

1. *Voice, clarity and consistency of the copy as written.*
2. *Whether the offerings now sit in the right places, and are named and described the right way. See **Where the offerings appear** at the end.*

*Audience: owners of Philippine companies with 20–100 people, frustrated with software that hasn't delivered. Tone: plain, direct, owner-to-owner. A few local details (Viber, ₱) are deliberate.*

## 7. FAQ

*Questions open and close on tap.*

## Questions owners ask us

**Our team doesn't fully use our software. What should we do?**
Find out why before you buy anything new. It's rarely your people; the software wasn't built around how they work. That's where we start.

**Do we really need AI?**
Maybe, maybe not. We look at how your team works first, then use AI where it pays off most, whether that's reading documents, answering customers or spotting trends. If it won't pay off, we'll tell you.

**What exactly do you build?**
Custom software, automation, AI integration and legacy system migration. Every build includes onboarding for your team and 90 days of hypercare after go-live. After that, ongoing support is quoted up front.

**You say you work alongside our team. What does that actually mean?**
We don't build from a requirements document in our office. We spend time where your team works, in person where we can, and watch how the job really gets done. We do all the building. Your team's only job is to tell us honestly whether it's what they need. Some call this forward-deployed engineering. We call it building with you.

**Will this pull my team away from their work?**
Barely. We do the building; your team just tells us what's working and what isn't. We keep their time with us as short as possible.

**How much will it cost?**
The first call is free. Deeper Dive on your processes is ₱10,000, fixed. The build is priced per outcome, and you see the number before you commit.

**What happens to our data?**
That depends on your setup and what we agree, and you'll know exactly what happens to it before we start. We sign an NDA before the Deeper Dive begins.

**How long does it take?**
It depends on what we build. Deeper Dive ends with a committed timeline, so you know before you spend on the build.

*Decisions for the reviewer:*

- ***Legacy system migration*** *is on the card, in the FAQ and in the search description, but not in the hero, the footer or any door tag. Leaving it out of the doors was a deliberate choice; the hero and footer were kept to three offerings for length.*
- ***Naming:*** *"AI integration" is used everywhere. Earlier drafts said "AI tools".*
- ***AI positioning:*** *every mention now says AI goes "where it pays off most". The FAQ keeps the honest fallback: "If it won't pay off, we'll tell you."*

## Notes on consistency

- ***Terms in use:*** *"Discovery Call" is step 01, the free 45-minute call. "Deeper Dive" is step 02, the paid ₱10,000 process review. Keep these names exact if any copy changes.*
- ***One call to action:*** *every booking button says "Book a Discovery Call". Door buttons are the only exception: they're problem-specific on purpose.*
- ***Repeated lines:***
  - *"45 minutes / free / with the team that will actually build for you" now appears only in the booking section. Step 01 says "Free, 45 minutes, no obligation."*
  - *The hero positioning line appears again, word for word, in the footer. Both now say "practical AI" where the rest of the page says "AI integration".*

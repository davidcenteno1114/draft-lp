Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information Ismoo collects through ismoo.tech, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it. It applies to this website and to the conversations that start from it.

Who we are

Ismoo ([REGISTERED BUSINESS NAME]) is a technology consultancy based in Makati, Philippines. We are the personal information controller for the information described here. You can reach us at letsbuild@ismoo.tech.

What we collect

When you fill in the booking form, we collect:

  • your name, company and email address;
  • what you tell us is getting in your way;
  • how you found us, if you choose to say;
  • which of the problems on our page you picked, if any.

When you book a time, our scheduling provider also receives your name, email and chosen time. If you email us or speak with us on a call, we keep what you share in that conversation.

This website does not use analytics or advertising trackers and does not set cookies of its own. [UPDATE THIS IF YOU ADD ANALYTICS.]

Why we use it

  • To schedule and prepare for your Discovery Call.
  • To reply to you and follow up on what we discussed.
  • If we work together, to carry out the Deeper Dive and any project we agree on.

We use your information because you asked us to contact you, and, if we work together, to perform our agreement with you. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Who we share it with

  • Google Fonts, which serves this site's typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google's servers, which receive your IP address.
  • [OTHER PROVIDERS, E.G. EMAIL HOSTING]

We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Your rights

  • receive a copy of it in a commonly used format;
  • be compensated for damages caused by inaccurate, unlawfully obtained or misused information;

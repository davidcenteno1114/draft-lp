Questions owners ask us

Our team doesn't fully use our software. What should we do?

Find out why before you buy anything new. It's rarely your people; the software wasn't built around how they work. That's where we start.

Do we really need AI?

Maybe, maybe not. We look at how your team works first, then use AI where it pays off most, whether that's reading documents, answering customers or spotting trends. If it won't pay off, we'll tell you.

What exactly do you build?

Custom software, automation, AI integration and legacy system migration. Every build includes onboarding for your team and 90 days of hypercare after go-live. After that, ongoing support is quoted up front.

You say you work alongside our team. What does that actually mean?

We don't build from a requirements document in our office. We spend time where your team works, in person where we can, and watch how the job really gets done. We do all the building. Your team's only job is to tell us honestly whether it's what they need. Some call this forward-deployed engineering. We call it building with you.

Will this pull my team away from their work?

Barely. We do the building; your team just tells us what's working and what isn't. We keep their time with us as short as possible.

How much will it cost?

The first call is free. Deeper Dive on your processes is ₱10,000, fixed. The build is priced per outcome, and you see the number before you commit.

What happens to our data?

That depends on your setup and what we agree, and you'll know exactly what happens to it before we start. We sign an NDA before the Deeper Dive begins.

How long does it take?

It depends on what we build. Deeper Dive ends with a committed timeline, so you know before you spend on the build.
